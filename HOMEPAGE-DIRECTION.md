# Homepage direction

## Mode and purpose

Persuade. A visitor should recognize a local HVAC and plumbing team, see the four technicians as people, understand the core services, and choose either Request Service or Call within the first viewport.

## Visual system

Editorial regional service photography paired with the official sailboat identity. Harbor Navy anchors the navigation and action areas; Warm Ivory is used sparingly as a light ground; Sea Teal guides links and section accents; Antique Gold reserves attention for the primary request action; Signal Red is limited to urgent phone/service cues. Libre Baskerville carries the main headings, DM Sans carries reading and controls. Square to lightly rounded geometry, visible rules, and typographic spacing replace repetitive card tiles and decorative badges.

## First viewport

A compact, clear header holds the full logo, service navigation, phone, and request action. Below it, the direct heating/cooling/plumbing headline and local service sentence lead into a full-width four-person studio photograph. The image retains its natural proportions so every technician remains visible. A thin trust strip begins immediately below, using real service facts and no invented numbers.

## Visitor path

Hero → factual trust points → scan six service routes → meet the team through actual work imagery → understand the three service steps → read a real review → confirm service area → call or request service.

## Signature interaction

The reviews control changes one verified quote at a time, with accessible labels and a visible position indicator. The mobile navigation opens as a readable drawer and can close by button, backdrop, or Escape.

## Scope and risk

Only the homepage markup and its scoped styles change in this stage. The other eleven pages keep their current content and layout. The generated group photograph is an identity-preserving approximation based on reference sheets, and should receive a human identity check before publication.
