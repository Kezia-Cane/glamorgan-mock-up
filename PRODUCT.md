# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners in New York's Capital Region who need heating, cooling, plumbing, ductless, or indoor-air service. They need to understand the services, judge whether Glamorgan serves their area, and request help or call quickly, including from a phone.

## Product Purpose

Present Glamorgan Heating & Cooling as a credible local service company and guide visitors to a request-service or phone action.

## Operating Context

This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript reference site. Its homepage will later be reconstructed as native editable Elementor content through NovaMira. The contact page currently has no local submission endpoint.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the twelve local page routes and working navigation. The current request is scoped to the homepage. The homepage must work at 1440 × 900, 768 × 1024, and 390 × 844, and be made from semantic text, images, links, and simple components. Do not invent service guarantees, pricing, testimonials, licensing, or temporary offers.

## Brand Commitments

Use the official Glamorgan full logo and ship crest favicon. The approved palette is Harbor Navy, Sea Teal, Antique Gold, Warm Ivory, and Signal Red. Headlines use Libre Baskerville; body and interface use DM Sans. Four supplied technician sheets are identity and uniform references for a studio-style group photograph. Keep their faces as close to the references as possible, and do not place the sheets or their labels on the site.

## Evidence on Hand

The project includes prior live-site screenshots, a service-area map, existing review quotations, official logo and favicon files, and four technician reference sheets supplied in the user's brief. The current published phone number, Facebook URL, and Instagram URL were checked against Glamorgan's homepage and contact page on 2026-11-05. The request does not provide a live form endpoint.

## Product Principles

- Show the people and real work behind the service.
- Make request and phone actions clear on every device.
- Use only verified business details and review copy.
- Keep the structure practical to rebuild as editable Elementor content.
